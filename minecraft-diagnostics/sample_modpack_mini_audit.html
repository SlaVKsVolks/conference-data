Minecraft Log + Modlist Mini-Audit

This sample shows the shape of a mini-audit when both the crash log and modlist are relevant.

Likely Issue Category

Mixed loader/version risk plus one missing dependency cluster. The modlist suggests several jars may not match the active NeoForge 1.20.1 instance.

Suspect Cluster

  • example-storage-fabric-1.20.1.jar appears in a NeoForge instance.
  • library-core is referenced by the log but missing from the modlist.
  • visualwidgets throws a Mixin failure after dependency resolution fails.

Prioritized Checklist

  1. Backup the current mods folder before changing jars.
  2. Replace Fabric-only jars with NeoForge builds or remove them.
  3. Add the required library dependency for the same Minecraft version.
  4. Relaunch and compare the new first fatal/error block.

No full modpack rebuild is included in the mini-audit. No guaranteed fix.
